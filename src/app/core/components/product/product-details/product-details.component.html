Next-Level Performance for Demanding Networks

The Archer AX6000 is designed for homes with heavy internet usage and multiple devices. Next-gen 802.11ax Wi-Fi technology delivers faster speeds, greater capacity, and reduced network congestion.

Ultimate WiFi 6 Speed

With WiFi 6 technology, the Archer AX6000 delivers combined speeds of up to 6000 Mbps - 1148 Mbps on the 2.4 GHz band and 4804 Mbps on the 5 GHz band. This means smoother streaming, faster downloads, and lag-free gaming.

Powerful Processing

A 1.8 GHz Quad-Core CPU and 3 co-processors ensure your router can handle multiple tasks and devices simultaneously without slowing down.

Expanded Connectivity

With 8 Gigabit LAN ports, you can connect more wired devices like game consoles, PCs, and TVs. The USB 3.0 and USB-C ports allow for easy media sharing and storage access.

Advanced Security

TP-Link HomeCare™ provides cutting-edge network and IoT protection. Features include robust parental controls, QoS, and antivirus powered by Trend Micro™.
